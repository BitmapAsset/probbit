Each row: branch A on top, branch B below, on a fixed 0 to 1 scale. the level the stance took (the most likely stance that keeps every habit). Traits whose level differs are named in cyan. The odds are marginals of the persona's own score model, not predictions about people.

Disable inertia: do the stances still differ?

Moods carry part of the last turn into the next (each mood's inertia). Rerun both branches with --no-inertia: the earlier events still happen, but no mood is carried.
