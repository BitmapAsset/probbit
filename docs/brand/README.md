# probbit brand

- The name is one word, lowercase: **probbit** (never "ProbBit", never two words). The probabilistic bit it is built from is a **p-bit** (plural p-bits).
- The lockup (`lockup-probbit.png`) is the rabbit mark (`logo.svg`, font-free) beside the word in Avenir Next 700, white on ink `#0b0e14`, with a cyan `#00F0FF` dot on the i; on light grounds the word is ink.
- Clear space: keep at least the mark's ear width (about a third of the mark's height) empty on every side of the lockup.
- Minimum sizes: mark 24 px tall on screen, word 22 px; below that use the mark alone. Do not stretch, recolour the body, or put the arrow inside the name.
